About Us

Dila Cakes & Treats is a food and baking brand offering beautifully made cakes, baked treats, desserts and our signature DFC – Dila Fried Chicken. Every item is prepared to order, so whatever the occasion, we work with you to create something you'll love.

From birthdays and celebrations to everyday cravings, we're here to bring delicious food to your table — with custom orders always welcome.
